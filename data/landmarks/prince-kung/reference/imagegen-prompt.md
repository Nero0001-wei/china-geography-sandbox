## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a TALL PORTRAIT multi-view Blender reference board, EXACTLY 6 equal rows and exactly 3 equal columns. Each row depicts one and the same Chinese geographical landmark three times, in LEFT a near-level front elevation, CENTER a 90-degree right side elevation, RIGHT a 45-degree elevated front-right view. Keep the sculpture, architectural forms, height, number of floors, colors and silhouette the same across all three views in each row; prioritize fidelity to the named real-world landmark. Existing modern tabletop miniatures for a national map, realistic surface and color, use clean detail that can be translated to low-poly 3D geometry. Faces, materials and neutral daylight softly lit on plain warm beige background with small discrete bases. Center each miniature in its cell with generous cropping space. No labels or text, no people, no decorative frames, no perspective consistency violations. The EXACT row order from the top: 1. National Museum of China west façade as it stands after 2011, very long symmetrical light warm-gray masonry building, rectangular open front colonnade of massive squared piers, wide shallow axial steps, regular multi-storey windows along wings and recessed rear volume. 2. Prince Kung's Mansion Beijing Yin'an Hall as now reconstructed, five-bay palace with glazed green hipped-gable roof, red columns, pale stone steps, low flanking halls and courtyard. 3. Shaanxi History Museum original Xiaozhai main campus facade: Tang-inspired peaked dark gray tile double roof, off-white masonry, red columns, lower symmetrical pavilion wings and broad entry stairs. 4. Xi'an city wall at Yongning Gate south front: historic multi-level gatehouse, outer archer tower, barbican and lower gate enclosure, pale gray brick and crenellations, architectural through-open archways and short drawbridge over the moat. 5. Yinxu ancestral temple and palace archaeological site at Xiaotun, Anyang: existing onsite reconstructed Shang hall with huge low four-slope thatched roof, open wooden-column porch and surrounding low rectangular ochre rammed-earth foundations. 6. Wuyuan Huangling hanging village: four or five terraced Hui-style houses with white horse-head gables, charcoal tiled roofs, warm wood racks with flat red and yellow crop-filled bamboo drying trays.
