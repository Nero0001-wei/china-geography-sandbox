## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a tall portrait 5-row × 3-column reference contact sheet of accurate, readable historic-site miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Visible geometry must conform to the real structures. Each row is a separate landmark, represented by exactly the SAME geometry three times, in a front, 90-degree right side, then 45-degree front-right orthographic view. Center each model on a small unobtrusive matte base and warm neutral background. Diffuse daylight, real color and material, simplified geometric shapes suitable for Blender reconstruction. Equal row and column spacing, each model fully inside its cell, no labels, no print decoration, no grid lines. Keep the models separated and avoid anything outside their respective cells. The strict row order from the top: 1. Temple of Heaven Hall of Prayer, three deep-blue circular roofs with a gilded finial and white three-level circular terrace. 2. Yungang Grotto 20, giant light sandstone seated Buddha exposed inside a dark tan cliff niche. 3. Mount Wutai Tayuan Temple, huge bell-shaped white stupa body and tapering gold finial with low red temple courtyard. 4. Shaolin Temple main gate, three-bay gray tiled roof, red wall, genuine recessed central entry. 5. Leshan Giant Buddha cut into a red riverside cliff, seated Buddha's hands on knees and narrow steps beside it
