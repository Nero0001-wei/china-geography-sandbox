## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Ordos Xiangshawan: crisp sweeping dune ridge in the Kubuqi Desert, several orange-gold sloping slip faces. 2. Mount Wuyi Jade Maiden Peak: red sandstone mushroom tower beside green Nine-Bend River, a tiny bamboo raft. 3. Shennongding ridge: long grassy summit with dark fir forest strips, layers of steep mountain gullies, tiny summit stele. 4. Detian waterfall, China-side viewpoint: stepped wide white cascade with multiple ribbons over lush dark karst terraces into river. 5. Wulong Tianlong Bridge: very high solid pale karst rock ribs across a steep canyon with genuine through opening and tiny historic inn below.
