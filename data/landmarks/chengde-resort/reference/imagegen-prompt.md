## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Macau A-Ma Temple: small historic green tiled shrine among a rocky slope, stone gateway and steps. 2. Beijing Summer Palace Tower of Buddhist Incense: towering octagonal ochre and green pagoda on big stone terrace among wing galleries. 3. Chengde Mountain Resort Shuixinxie: three linked gray pavilions across the water with low stone banks. 4. Pingyao old city market tower: multi-roofed tall gold-green pavilion over a genuine archway above the dark gray tiled street. 5. Humble Administrator's Garden at Suzhou: Yuanxiang Hall, low lotus pond, white garden walls and delicate gray roof gallery.
