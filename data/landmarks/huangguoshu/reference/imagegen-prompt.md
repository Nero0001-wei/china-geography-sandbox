## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Tianya Haijiao, Sanya: two signature broad warm granite boulders by a cream sand beach and translucent turquoise wave edge. 2. Golden Summit of Mount Emei: steep gray summit cliff, white stepped platform and golden Buddhist shrine above thick evergreen trees. 3. Huangguoshu Waterfall: wide segmented curtain of flowing white water over rounded covered rock face into a jade-green plunge pool. 4. Jade Dragon Snow Mountain: black and pale-gray ragged steep alpine peaks with ribbon glacier and sharply defined snowline. 5. Everest north face from Rongbuk: vast steep symmetrical pyramid summit with black and white strata, bare Tibetan moraine and tiny monastery.
