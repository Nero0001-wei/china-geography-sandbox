## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Wudang Mountain Golden Hall: small dark-gold shrine perched on massive slanted gray rock and summit walls. 2. Yueyang Tower by Lake Dongting: three storeys of red wood columns and gold helmet-shaped roof on gray waterfront ramparts. 3. Dazu Baodingshan Rock Carvings, Nirvana of the Buddha: immense reclining sandstone Buddha and attendants in a concave cliff. 4. Jiayuguan Fort: tan rammed-earth double walls and red-brown three-storey gatehouse with black tiled roofs. 5. Western Xia Imperial Mausoleums no. 3, Ningxia: weathered yellow earthen octagonal tiered mound in dry gravel with low ruins.
