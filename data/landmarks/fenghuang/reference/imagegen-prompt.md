## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Hong Bridge in Fenghuang ancient town: two-storey red columns, green roofs, covered bridge over the Tuo River, flanked by stilt houses. 2. Zili village Mingshilou, Kaiping: narrow five-story pale gray fortified tower with top arches, corner turrets and field terrace. 3. Haikou Zhongshan Road arcade houses: pastel South Seas rowhouses, decorative parapets and ground floor half-circle archways. 4. Kashgar Old City east gate: tawny earthen gatehouse and narrow towers around tapered pointed arch with Uyghur geometric ornaments. 5. Tiananmen Square north end: recognizable long red and ochre Tiananmen Gate, stone bridges and light gray paved forecourt.
