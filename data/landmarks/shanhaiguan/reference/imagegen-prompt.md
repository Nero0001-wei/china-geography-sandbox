## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Shanhaiguan First Pass under Heaven: broad gray-brick crenellations and a two-storey dark tiled Chinese gatehouse. 2. Shenyang Imperial Palace Dazheng Hall: low octagonal gold-tile pavilion with green trim and symmetrical flag pavilions. 3. Puji Temple on Putuo Mountain: yellow-and-red historic Buddhist hall by Haiyin pond and a low stone bridge. 4. Tengwang Pavilion in Nanchang: very tall central red column and dark green roof pavilion with symmetrical stepped wings. 5. Confucius Temple Dacheng Hall, Qufu: monumental ochre twin-eaved roof resting on carved pale stone dragon columns.
