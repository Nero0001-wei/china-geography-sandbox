## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Qingdao Zhanqiao: very long slender gray stone causeway ending in an octagonal two-storey green roof pavilion above the ocean. 2. Orange Isle Young Mao sculpture at Changsha: recognizable side profile and windswept hair rendered in horizontal granite bands on grassy narrow island tip. 3. Sanxingdui museum new building: broad bronze colored sloping trapezoidal contemporary building and small Sanxingdui bronze mask in foreground. 4. National Palace Museum in Taipei main building: symmetrical cream palace on raised pale terraces with wide turquoise green tiled roofs. 5. Yalu River Broken Bridge: rust-gray open steel trusses on stone piers, broken end extending into water from one bank.
