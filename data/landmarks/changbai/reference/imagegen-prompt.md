## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Morigele River, Hulunbuir grassland: tight S-shaped narrow teal meanders winding across buttery-green meadow and low hills. 2. Changbai Mountain's Tianchi: distinctive nearly round cobalt crater lake, encircling jagged charcoal mountain wall and patches of snow. 3. Mount Lu Three-Step Spring: three cascading white washes in steep narrow weathered tan-green canyon. 4. Yuanjiajie Wulingyuan: tightly grouped tall slender flat-topped sandstone pillars with small pines, separated by genuine deep gaps. 5. Xingping Yellow Cloth Shoal in Yangshuo: consecutive rounded cone karst peaks lining still broad blue-green Li River.
