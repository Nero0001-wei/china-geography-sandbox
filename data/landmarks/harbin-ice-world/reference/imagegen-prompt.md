## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Nanjing Museum history hall: ochre glazed double eaves and tall slate-colored Liao-revival entrance pediment on wide pale podium. 2. Jingdezhen Imperial Kiln Museum: groups of alternating high and low red brick barrel vaults with openings and a reflecting pool. 3. Harbin Ice and Snow World: translucent frosty ice-blue tiered central ice spire with smaller ice towers and steps. 4. Guangzhou Chimelong Safari Park: wooded zoo enclosure with a clearly recognizable white tiger, warm weather path and discrete low entrance. 5. Kaifeng Millennium City Park: high arched Song Dynasty wooden Rainbow Bridge with green Bian River, one little wooden boat, and compact tiled shops.
