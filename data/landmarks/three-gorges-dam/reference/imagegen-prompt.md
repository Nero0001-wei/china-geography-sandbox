## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Shanghai Oriental Pearl Tower: tall skinny steel spire, two main red-purple spheres, gray branching tripod legs. 2. Three Gorges Dam: long pale concrete gravity dam with repeating spillway gates and contrasting Yangtze water levels. 3. Dujiangyan Fish Mouth in Sichuan: wedge shaped gray rocky levee splitting jade-green river into two water channels. 4. Victoria Harbour Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront miniature: narrow red brick historic clock tower, pier edge and green-and-white Star Ferry in water. 5. Tianjin Eye: white Ferris wheel with tiny capsules and genuine open spokes mounted on red and white Yongle Bridge above the river.
