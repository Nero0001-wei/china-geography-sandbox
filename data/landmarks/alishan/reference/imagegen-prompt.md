## 完整提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Tall portrait reference contact sheet, precisely 5 rows and 3 columns. Accurate readable physical diorama miniatures for a national tabletop geography map. Every row is a different site spelled out below, showing exactly the same mini 3D structure from three orthographic views, on the left near-horizontal front, in the middle 90-degree right side, on the right 45-degree elevated front-right. Each model fully inside its regular cell, with enough margin to crop, small unobtrusive matte base, soft daylight and plain warm sand background. Keep distinctive materials and structure consistent across the 3 views. Use simple legible silhouettes and realistic colors suitable for actual low-poly Blender reconstruction. No captions, no numbers, no visible guides, no framing. Strict row order top to bottom: 1. Fanjingshan Red Clouds Golden Summit: thrilling deeply split tall rocky twin peak, two tiny historic temples linked by a bridge over the crack. 2. Kunming Stone Forest Ashima: tall blade-like eroded dark-gray limestone pillars shaped by karst, sparse green ground plants. 3. Zhangye Danxia: striped slanting red, gold, pale cream and dark amber layered ridges with a deeply eroded profile. 4. Shapotou in Ningxia: massive warm desert dune right at a curve of turbid Yellow River, narrow green riverbank. 5. Alishan Zhaoping: narrow red forest railway train curved on dark rails among tall slender green cypress on elevated Taiwanese slope.
